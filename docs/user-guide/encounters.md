# Encounters

## What Is An Encounter?

An encounter records one clinical contact or care episode for a patient. Use an
encounter for an outpatient visit, telehealth or telephone contact, portal
contact, emergency contact, or inpatient stay.

An encounter gives related clinical work a shared context. It includes the
contact type, purpose, status, title, time, clinician, and location. An
inpatient admission and discharge are recorded as the start and end of one
inpatient encounter.

## Notes And Other Records

An encounter can include notes. Typed notes and voice transcripts belong to the
encounter and appear in its details. You can review and edit the current note
while working in the encounter.

Other clinical records are kept separate, even when they arise from the same
contact:

- Laboratory results are diagnostic reports with their measured observations.
- Imaging studies retain their own performed time and study identity.
- Uploaded files are clinical documents with their attachments.
- Immunizations, medications, and procedures are separate clinical records.

The patient history shows these records together in chronological order. Opening
an encounter displays its encounter-specific detail, including notes, summaries,
voice-note work, and chart-review draft support.

## Create An Encounter

1. Open the patient record.
2. Select **New Encounter**.
3. Enter the encounter type, purpose, status, title, and start time.
4. Add the clinician, location, or description when they are available.
5. Select **Create Encounter**.

## Review An Encounter

1. Open the patient record.
2. Select an encounter in the history.
3. Review its summary and notes.
4. Use the available detail actions to update the note, work with voice capture,
   or request chart-review draft support.

An encounter is a clinical record, not a diagnosis or treatment instruction.
Folium's generated summaries and chart-review output are draft support that
require human review.
